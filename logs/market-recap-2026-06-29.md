# Market Recap — Monday, June 29, 2026

*Risk-on to start a holiday-shortened week: Iran de-escalation over the weekend put a bid under stocks, the morning gap got sold into a ~10:15am washout, and then it was a one-way grind to close on the highs. The Dow closed above 52,000 for the first time on the same day Alphabet joined the index. Tech and a red-hot space/satellite theme led; breadth, though, was narrow.*

The catalyst was geopolitical relief: over the weekend the U.S. and Iran agreed to "stand down," talks were set to resume Tuesday in Qatar, and Iran was to receive ~$6B in frozen assets. VIX melted from 18.5 to a 17.5 handle.

## 2. How the Day Traded — the play-by-play

- **Overnight / pre-market (4:00–9:30am):** Futures firm on the Iran de-escalation headlines; ES gapped up ~+0.9%. SPY drifted 736–738 pre-market, QQQ 714–716. Calm, grinding-higher overnight tape.
- **9:30–9:45am — gap-up open, quick pop:** SPY opened ~738.7, QQQ ~716.8. NVDA and IWM tagged their highs of the morning right at 9:45 — the knee-jerk relief spike.
- **9:45–10:15am — sell the open, the washout:** The gap got faded. The whole board rolled over into a **session low at ~10:15am** — SPY 732.09, QQQ flushed all the way to **705.16 (a full gap-fill)**, VIX spiked to **19.45** (peak fear of the day, exactly at the equity low). High-beta semis took the worst of it on thin morning liquidity (INTC, MU, AMD, MRVL, CRWV all printed sharp wicks). No discrete catalyst — this was a classic gap-up-fade / shakeout: the easy Iran-relief gap got sold, then reversed. (No data surprise on the tape; the Dallas Fed survey was the only morning release.)
- **10:15am–12:00pm — the V-bottom and reclaim:** From the 10:15 low it was straight back up. QQQ reclaimed 715 by 11:00 and 717+ by 11:30; SPY worked back over its open. The 10:15 wash proved to be *the* low of the day — nothing revisited it.
- **12:00–2:00pm — the trend leg:** Relentless, low-volatility grind. SPY 738.9 → 740.6, QQQ 719.6 → 723. Semis (SOX) and the space complex led; VIX bled down through 18 to 17.7.
- **~2:00–3:35pm — Tesla takes the baton:** TSLA accelerated into the afternoon, tagging **$413 by 3:35pm** (+8.5%) after the NHTSA closed its three-year power-steering probe and analysts hiked Q2 delivery estimates. The index just plateaued at the highs (SPY pinned ~740.6).
- **3:45–4:00pm — strong close, no fade:** Buyers pressed into the bell. SPY high 741.56 and QQQ 724.57 both at **3:55pm**; GOOGL, TSM, MRVL, WDC, INTC, ASTS all closed at/near session highs. Closing on the highs with a Dow milestone — about as strong a finish as the tape offers.
- **After-hours:** Quiet, gains held. RKLB kept climbing (98.0 → 101.2), MSFT firmed slightly off its weak close (368.5 → 371.3).

## 4. S/R Report Card

**Cleanest holds / reads:**
- **QQQ — PDL 702.82 held at the lows.** The 10:15 washout dropped QQQ to 705.16, slicing through PML 712.05 and even PDC 706.69 (full gap-fill) — but it stopped *right* above prior-day low 702.82 and reversed. The single best support read of the day.
- **MSFT — rejected at PMH 380.90.** High of 380.50 at 9:50am, failed exactly there, rolled over and closed red below PDC. Textbook resistance rejection.
- **BE — PDH 298.62 (prior spike) held as a ceiling.** Rallied to 283.52 but the PDH was never challenged; closed 275.
- **SPY — PMH/PDH flipped to support.** After the morning fade undercut PML 735.14 (low 732.09), SPY reclaimed and broke cleanly above PMH 738.03 and PDH 736.52, closing 740.89 above both.

**Broke easily / failed:**
- **TSLA and TSM** sliced through PDH and PMH like they weren't there (TSLA past 387.80/384.59; TSM past 436.13/440.28) — pure momentum, levels offered no friction.
- **GOOGL** broke PDH 346.36 / PMH 341.90 on Dow-inclusion flows.
- **AAPL — failed breakout.** Opening pop above PMH 285.20 (high 288.30 @ 9:30) immediately failed; faded all day back to ≈PDC (281.66).

**Gap fills (broad gap-UP day):**
- **QQQ filled its gap and went** — tagged 705.16 (below PDC 706.69) at the 10:15 low, then rallied 2.5%. Classic fill-and-go.
- **SPY gap held** — low 732.09 stayed a whisker above PDC 731.23; never filled.
- **INTC** gapped up, then flushed hard to 118.51 (deep over-fill) before a full V-recovery to close green (+3%).

## 6. Bottom Line

1. **Shape first:** gap-up on Iran relief → **sell-the-open washout into ~10:15am** (VIX 19.45, QQQ filled its gap) → **one-way grind to close on the highs.** Strongest kind of tape, capped by the Dow's first close above 52,000. When the 10:15 low held, the day was decided.
2. **Leadership was semis + a space/satellite frenzy** (RKLB–Iridium $8B sparked a sector-wide re-rating) plus TSLA's +8.5% on the NHTSA probe closure and GOOGL's Dow debut. SOX +3.8%.
3. **But mind the breadth:** RUT/IWM flat, AAPL flat, MSFT red, crypto miners down. This was mega-cap/theme-driven, not a broad melt-up — the kind of narrow strength that can mean-revert.

**Watch tomorrow (Tue, Jun 30 — quarter/month-end):** the **U.S.–Iran talks in Qatar** (the whole risk-on premise rests on them staying "on track"); whether semis and the space complex **follow through or give back** a big day; whether small-caps (RUT) finally confirm; and **quarter-end rebalancing flows**, which can distort the tape into the close.
