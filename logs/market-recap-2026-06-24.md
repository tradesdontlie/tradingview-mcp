## 2. How the Day Traded — the play-by-play

**Overnight / pre-market —** This was the third straight session of an AI-memory bloodbath (Nasdaq −2% Mon, KOSPI ~−10% and Samsung/SK Hynix crushed Tue on a BofA rate-hike note). Wednesday set up as a stabilization attempt: QQQ gapped up +0.34%, VIX gapped down, and the beaten-down semis (AMD, MU, QCOM, WDC, SNDK) all gapped *up* in the morning markup — a relief-bid open ahead of Micron after the close.

**9:30 — split open.** Indices opened higher (SPY 736.84, QQQ 717.68), but the speculative complex told the real story: RKLB, OUST, IONQ, RGTI, ASTS, IREN, NBIS all printed their **session highs at the 9:30 bell** and never looked back — the open was the high for the whole high-beta book.

**9:30–11:00 — the melt-up.** Clean push to the highs: SPY 739.94 (11:00), QQQ 719.91 (11:00), IWM 299.69 (11:15), VIX bottoming 18.04 (11:00). And the rally died *exactly* at resistance — **SPY tagged PDH 739.63 and rejected; QQQ tagged PMH 719.27 and rejected.** Textbook level rejections (see Report Card).

**11:00–1:00pm — rollover.** Indices eased off the highs in a slow drift; underneath, the speculative names kept bleeding the entire time. No catalyst — just supply.

**~1:30pm — the cliff.** The sharp risk-off leg: SPY 736→732, QQQ 714→708, and **VIX spiked from ~18.8 to 20.1 (RTH high 20.34)**. No discrete headline — this was de-grossing/de-risking ahead of Micron after the close, the marquee read on the whole AI-memory trade after a week of carnage. COIN bottomed 1:45pm, CRCL/FLY/NBIS made lows 1:30–1:55pm.

**1:30–3:10pm — grind to the lows.** Session lows printed in a cluster right at **~3:10pm**: SPY 730.84, QQQ 704.47, NVDA 196.59, MU 991.1, TSLA 373.05 — and the entire speculative complex bottomed there together. Pure de-risking into the print.

**3:10–4:00pm — bounce into the bell.** Short-covering/positioning lifted it off the lows: SPY closed 733.16, QQQ 710.7, NVDA back to 199.2. Close: S&P −0.10%, Nasdaq −0.43%, **Dow +0.35% green** on the rotation into IBM/Merck/J&J.

**After the bell — the rescue (two stories).**
- **Micron blew the doors off (~4:20pm):** EPS $25.11 vs ~$20.2, revenue $41.5B vs $35.8B (revenue ~4×'d YoY), Q4 guide **$50B** vs $43B est., gross margin ~86%, plus **$100B in strategic customer agreements** and $22B of customer cash prepayments. Stock **+16% AH**. Read-through ripped the memory/storage/semis complex: **WDC +12.3%, SNDK +12.3%, MRVL +5.3%, CRDO +5.3%, INTC +5.3%, CRWV +4.1%, AMD +3.8%, TSM +3.8%, NVDA +0.7%.** QQQ +1.9% AH, VIX −4.6% AH.
- **Qualcomm investor day (separate catalyst): QCOM +12.8% AH** — doubled its non-handset revenue target to $40B by FY29 (incl. $15B data center), unveiled AI data-center chips/servers with **Microsoft and Meta** as named customers (Meta multigen deal for the Dragonfly C1000 CPU), and is buying AI-software startup Modular.
