## 2. How the Day Traded

**Overnight / pre-market —** Soft. Asia sank (Kospi/Nikkei down), the AI-capex/"OpenAI IPO delay" theme leaned on semis, and oil bled ~3.5% on unimpeded Hormuz traffic. **8:30am PCE**: headline 4.1% y/y (hot) but a smaller-than-expected monthly gain — net a marginal relief (gold rallied). SPY premarket 727–732 (PMH 732.62); QQQ heavy at 705–712.

**9:30–9:40 — gap down, tech worst.** QQQ opened 707 and immediately flushed to its session low **702.82, slicing the PML (705.14)**. SPY opened 728.9 right on its PML (728.50); VIX spiked to 20.72. The open was the low for the day in the big proxies.

**9:40–11:40 — the relief grind (the day's high).** Steady bid all morning: SPY 728 → **736.52 high @11:40**, QQQ clawed 702.82 → **715.55 @11:40**, IWM 296 → 300. VIX bled 20.7 → ~19. Healthcare ripped (LLY to a fresh ATH, MRNA exploding on its Science Day / FDA flu-panel win), and COIN/HOOD caught a crypto relief bid (Bitcoin stabilized ~$60K after Thursday's 20-month low). MU did a knee-jerk dip-buy pop to **1,198 @10:05** before rolling over.

**~11:40 — the rejection.** QQQ topped at **715.55 — literally into Thursday's close (PDC 715.84) and failed there.** No fresh catalyst; a clean technical rejection at prior-close resistance plus midday de-risking. That was the high-water mark.

**11:40–3:00 — slow afternoon fade, rotation engine running.** QQQ rolled 715.5 → 708, SPY 736 → 731. The bleed was all AI-infra/semis: **MU faded all session toward 1,127, BE collapsed double-digits, CRDO −10%, plus NBIS/MRVL/AVGO/SNDK heavy.** That cash went to healthcare (LLY) and crypto. VIX sat pinned ~18.7 the whole time — orderly, no panic. TSLA was the afternoon's fun one: spiked to **387.8 @11:35** on robotaxi-expansion momentum, then gave it *all* back.

**3:00–4:00 — mixed close.** SPY held 731; **IWM firmed into the bell** (small-cap relative strength) back to 298.3; QQQ slipped to 706.7, back near its PML. NVDA, after poking 195.54 (just over PMH 194.44) midday, was rejected and faded to close **191.99 — right on PDL/PML support (192.1)**. TSLA closed **379.32, dead on its PDH (379.24).**

**After hours —** Quiet. No major after-close earnings; most AH moves <1% (TWLO −1.8%, NTAP −1.7%, BE bounced +1.8% off the lows).
