## How the Day Traded — the play-by-play

**The setup (overnight):** Asian chips cratered — KOSPI hit a circuit-breaker (~−10%), SK Hynix/Samsung −12% on a report SK Hynix is **throttling HBM4 production** on softer Nvidia next-gen demand. Layered on top: BofA had flipped Monday (Jun 22, ~7:23am) to calling **three Fed HIKES in 2026** ("inflation unambiguously worse") under new hawkish Chair Warsh. US futures gapped down hard.

**9:30–10:20 — ripped out of the gate (gap-fill bounce).** Everything opened *off* the pre-market lows and ran hard for 50 minutes: QQQ +0.9% (716.9→723.6), SPY +0.8%, IWM +1.3% off the open, **MU +4%** off its open (1072→1125), COIN +3.4%, TSM to 447. VIX collapsed 20.5→19.1. **Why:** classic oversold dip-buy into a gap, fueled by **oil falling to a ~3-month low (~$73)** on Iran de-escalation (Treasury's 60-day Iranian crude license + Strait of Hormuz traffic reopening) — lower oil = lower inflation fear = risk-on. BofA's chip analyst (Arya) also lifted his Micron target into earnings, framing the dip as buyable.

**10:06am — the hot PMI prints.** S&P Global flash **Manufacturing PMI 55.7**, a multi-year high vs ~54.6 expected — fastest output/orders since 2021. A hot manufacturing number is *hawkish*: it re-armed the exact "Fed will hike / AI valuations too rich" fear BofA had flagged. The front-end repriced (2yr yield near a multi-month high).

**~10:20 — the cliff.** Within minutes of the PMI, the bounce died — and it died **exactly at the pre-market-high resistance**: SPY topped 739.63 vs morning PMH 739.20; QQQ topped 723.61 vs PMH 724.85; MU/TSM peaked at 10:20 too. From there it rolled over into ~11:15 (the day's worst 30-min stretch: QQQ −0.44%/−0.45%, MU −1.3%/−1.3%). A hot data print rejecting precisely at the level the morning markup drew — clean.

**11:15–1:35pm — midday stabilization.** The selling exhausted; indices drifted back up and **VIX made its session LOW (18.61) at 1:35pm** — fear actually drained out at midday. Looked like a base.

**1:45–2:55pm — second leg down to the lows.** The base failed and chips rolled to session lows around 2:50: MU 1038 @ 2:50, QQQ 712.1 @ 2:50, TSM 435.79 @ 2:55, TSLA 379 @ 2:55. **Why:** no discrete headline here — the 1:12pm 2-year auction was *fine* (bid-to-cover 2.64), no afternoon Fed speaker, no bearish Iran print. This leg was **positioning/de-risking** — chips bleeding ahead of **Micron's after-close earnings June 24** (options implied ~17% move) into a thin, quarter-end / pre-Russell-reconstitution (Jun 26) tape.

**3:55pm — weak close.** Sold into the bell across the board (sharpest 5-min drops of the day at 15:55); **NVDA closed dead on its low at 200.01.** Nobody wanted chip risk overnight into Micron.

Watch whether 200 holds and whether chip damage spreads or stays contained. Quarter-end + Russell reconstitution (Jun 26) keep the tape thin; Iran talks run through Jun 25 (oil-bearish so far).
