## trading rules

Condition Action Comments
PDH or R1 is broken with volume Take a Call Expect breakout continuation. Entry after candle closes above PDH/R1. Confirm with price above rising 20 EMA.
PDL or S1 is broken with volume Take a Put Bearish breakout; enter after breakdown with confirmation. Confirm with price below falling 20 EMA.
Price opens above CPR Bias: Bullish Look for long opportunities near VWAP, TC or R1. 20 EMA rising adds confidence.
Price opens below CPR Bias: Bearish Look for short near VWAP, BC or S1. 20 EMA falling confirms trend.
Price opens inside CPR Wait/Rangebound Avoid early trades. Wait for breakout of CPR. Good for reversal trades if price breaks CPR + 20 EMA confirms.
Price rejects TC with wick or pin bar Take a Put Use reversal confirmation + volume drop. CPR acts as resistance. Extra confirmation if 20 EMA is above price.
Price rejects BC with wick or pin bar Take a Call CPR acts as support. Use bullish price action for entry. Stronger if 20 EMA is below price.
Price breaks CPR from below with strength Take a Call Trend reversal signal. Entry above TC with SL below CPR. Confirm 20 EMA crossing above CPR.
Price breaks CPR from above with strength Take a Put Bearish reversal. Entry below BC with SL above CPR. Confirm with 20 EMA sloping down.
Narrow CPR + breakout above R1 Take a Call Trending day likely. Trail stop with higher CPR levels (R2, R3). 20 EMA must support price.
Narrow CPR + breakdown below S1 Take a Put Trending down day. Add confluence with RSI/MACD. 20 EMA should be falling above price.
Wide CPR + rejection at TC or BC Reversal trade Price likely to oscillate. Fade moves near edges. Avoid if price trends away from 20 EMA.
Price moves back inside CPR after breakout Fakeout — Reverse If breakout fails and price re-enters CPR, take trade in opposite direction. Confirm reversal with 20 EMA slope.
Virgin CPR from previous day being hit Reversal Setup Price may reverse or consolidate; watch for exhaustion candles. Extra confluence if near 20 EMA.
Price breaks CPR + VWAP support Take trade with trend CPR + VWAP alignment = strong directional confirmation. 20 EMA trend adds conviction.
Price opens far from CPR and moves toward it Reversion setup CPR acts as a magnet. Trade toward CPR if candles show direction and 20 EMA aligns with move.
